## Lesson

A **ratio** compares amounts of two or more things. If a drink is made with 1
cup of juice for every 4 cups of water, the ratio of juice to water is

$$1 : 4$$

read "one to four". The **order matters**: juice to water is $1 : 4$, but water
to juice is $4 : 1$.

### Simplifying ratios

A ratio works like a fraction: multiply or divide every part by the same
number and it means the same thing. To simplify, divide by the **HCF**:

$$12 : 18 = 2 : 3 \qquad (\text{divide by } 6)$$

The parts must be in the **same units** before you simplify. 40 cm to 2 m is
not $40 : 2$. Change 2 m to 200 cm first:

$$40 : 200 = 1 : 5$$

Get rid of decimals or fractions by multiplying: $1.5 : 2 = 3 : 4$ (multiply
by 2).

### Dividing a quantity in a given ratio

> Share \$360 between Ana and Ben in the ratio $4 : 5$.

1. **Add the parts** to find the total number of shares: $4 + 5 = 9$.
2. **Find one share:** $360 \div 9 = 40$.
3. **Multiply** each part by one share:
   - Ana: $4 \times 40 = \$160$
   - Ben: $5 \times 40 = \$200$

**Check:** $160 + 200 = 360$. ✓ The shares must add back to the whole.

It works the same with three parts. Divide 84 kg in the ratio $2 : 3 : 7$:
$2 + 3 + 7 = 12$ shares, one share is $84 \div 12 = 7$ kg, so the portions are
14 kg, 21 kg and 49 kg.

The classic mistake is dividing by one of the parts instead of their total —
$360 \div 4$ and $360 \div 5$ — which gives amounts that don't add up to 360.

### Working back from one share

Sometimes you're told one person's amount, not the total.

> Cake and bread flour are mixed in the ratio $3 : 5$. There are 750 g of
> bread flour. How much flour is there altogether?

The 5 parts of bread flour weigh 750 g, so one part is $750 \div 5 = 150$ g.
Altogether there are $3 + 5 = 8$ parts: $8 \times 150 = 1200$ g.

The key is always the same: find **one part**, then scale up.

### Ratios and fractions

A ratio can be turned into fractions of the whole. If boys to girls is
$2 : 3$, there are 5 parts, so

$$\text{boys} = \frac{2}{5} \text{ of the class}, \qquad \text{girls} = \frac{3}{5}$$

Note that the boys are $\frac{2}{5}$ of the class, **not** $\frac{2}{3}$.
$\frac{2}{3}$ compares boys with girls, not boys with the whole class.

## Flashcards

### 1.18 | How do you simplify a ratio?
Divide every part by their HCF. $15 : 25 = 3 : 5$.

Put the parts in the **same units** first.

### 1.18 | What are the three steps to divide a quantity in a given ratio?
1. **Add** the parts to get the total number of shares.
2. **Divide** the quantity by that total to find one share.
3. **Multiply** each part by one share.

### 1.18 | Share 50 in the ratio $2 : 3$.
$20$ and $30$.

$2 + 3 = 5$ shares, one share is $50 \div 5 = 10$.

### 1.18 | Boys to girls is $3 : 4$. What fraction of the class are boys?
$\frac{3}{7}$ — there are $3 + 4 = 7$ parts altogether.

> Hint: it is not $\frac{3}{4}$.

### 1.18 | Simplify 50 cents : \$2.
$1 : 4$. Change \$2 to 200 cents, then $50 : 200 = 1 : 4$.

## Questions

### 1.18 | mcq | 1 mark | difficulty 1
The ratio $24 : 36$ in its simplest form is

- A. $1 : 3$
- B. $2 : 3$
- C. $4 : 6$
- D. $3 : 2$

**Answer:** B

**Working:** The HCF of 24 and 36 is 12. $24 \div 12 = 2$ and $36 \div 12 = 3$,
giving $2 : 3$.

C is equivalent but not fully simplified. D reverses the order.

### 1.18 | numeric | 1 mark | difficulty 2
\$630 is shared between Kyle and Dana in the ratio $2 : 7$. How much does
Dana receive, in dollars?

**Answer:** 490

**Working:** $2 + 7 = 9$ shares. One share is $630 \div 9 = 70$. Dana gets
$7 \times 70 = 490$ dollars.

### 1.18 | numeric | 1 mark | difficulty 2
Concrete is mixed from cement, sand and gravel in the ratio $1 : 2 : 4$. How
many kilograms of sand are in 91 kg of concrete?

**Answer:** 26

**Working:** $1 + 2 + 4 = 7$ shares. One share is $91 \div 7 = 13$ kg. Sand is
2 shares: $2 \times 13 = 26$ kg.

### 1.18 | numeric | 1 mark | difficulty 3
The ratio of red to blue marbles in a bag is $5 : 3$. There are 18 blue
marbles. How many marbles are in the bag altogether?

**Answer:** 48

**Working:** 3 parts are 18 marbles, so one part is $18 \div 3 = 6$.
Altogether there are $5 + 3 = 8$ parts: $8 \times 6 = 48$ marbles.

### 1.18 | structured | 5 marks | difficulty 3
Mr Charles leaves \$12 000 to his three grandchildren, Ria, Sean and Tia, to
be shared in the ratio of their ages. Ria is 8, Sean is 12 and Tia is 20.

(a) Write the ratio of their ages in its simplest form. **(1 mark)**

(b) Calculate how much each grandchild receives. **(3 marks)**

(c) What fraction of the money does Tia receive? **(1 mark)**

**Answer:** (a) $2 : 3 : 5$ (b) Ria \$2400, Sean \$3600, Tia \$6000
(c) $\frac{1}{2}$

**Working:** (a) $8 : 12 : 20$. The HCF is 4, giving $2 : 3 : 5$.

(b) $2 + 3 + 5 = 10$ shares. One share is $12\,000 \div 10 = 1200$.
Ria: $2 \times 1200 = 2400$. Sean: $3 \times 1200 = 3600$. Tia:
$5 \times 1200 = 6000$. (1 mark for one share, 2 marks for all three amounts.)
Check: $2400 + 3600 + 6000 = 12\,000$. ✓

(c) Tia has 5 of the 10 shares: $\frac{5}{10} = \frac{1}{2}$.
