## Lesson

Not everyone can pay for a car, a fridge or a house all at once. Buying **by
instalments** lets you take the item now and pay over time — but you almost
always pay **more** than the cash price in total. The questions in this
lesson are about working out how much more.

### Hire purchase

On **hire purchase** (HP), you pay:

1. a **deposit** up front, then
2. a number of equal **instalments**, usually monthly.

$$\text{HP price} = \text{deposit} + (\text{number of instalments} \times \text{instalment})$$

> A fridge has a cash price of \$3 600. On hire purchase, it needs a 20%
> deposit and 12 monthly payments of \$280.

- Deposit: $0.20 \times 3600 = \$720$
- Instalments: $12 \times 280 = \$3360$
- HP price: $720 + 3360 = \$4080$

The buyer pays $4080 - 3600 = \$480$ **more** than the cash price. As a
percentage of the cash price, that's
$\frac{480}{3600} \times 100\% = 13\tfrac{1}{3}\%$.

That extra \$480 is really interest: the price of borrowing the money.

### Finding the instalment

Work backwards: take off the deposit, then share what's left equally.

> The HP price of a sofa is \$2 900. The deposit is \$500, and the rest is paid
> in 16 equal monthly instalments.

$$\text{Instalment} = \frac{2900 - 500}{16} = \frac{2400}{16} = \$150$$

### Mortgages

A **mortgage** is a long-term loan to buy property, repaid in monthly
instalments over many years — often 20 or 25. The idea is the same as hire
purchase, just much bigger.

> The Joseph family buys a house for \$450 000. They pay a 10% deposit and
> borrow the rest as a mortgage, repaid at \$3 100 a month for 25 years.

- Deposit: $0.10 \times 450\,000 = \$45\,000$
- Mortgage loan: $450\,000 - 45\,000 = \$405\,000$
- Total of repayments: $3100 \times 12 \times 25 = \$930\,000$
- Interest paid: $930\,000 - 405\,000 = \$525\,000$

Over 25 years, the family repays more than twice what they borrowed. That's
why a larger deposit or a shorter term can save so much money.

Watch the units: a monthly payment over **years** needs $\times 12$ to count the
payments. 25 years is 300 monthly payments.

## Flashcards

### 2.5 | How do you calculate the hire purchase price?
$$\text{HP price} = \text{deposit} + (\text{number of instalments} \times \text{instalment})$$

### 2.5 | How much extra does hire purchase cost?
The HP price minus the cash price.

### 2.5 | How do you find the size of each instalment?
Take the deposit off the HP price, then divide by the number of instalments.

### 2.5 | How many monthly payments are made on a 20-year mortgage?
$20 \times 12 = 240$ payments.

### 2.5 | What is a mortgage?
A long-term loan to buy property, repaid in monthly instalments over many
years.

## Questions

### 2.5 | numeric | 1 mark | difficulty 1
A television can be bought on hire purchase with a deposit of \$500 and 18
monthly instalments of \$135. Calculate the hire purchase price, in dollars.

**Answer:** 2930

**Working:** $500 + 18 \times 135 = 500 + 2430 = 2930$.

### 2.5 | numeric | 1 mark | difficulty 2
A washing machine costs \$4 800 cash. On hire purchase, a 25% deposit is paid,
and the total hire purchase price is \$5 400. The balance is paid in 12 equal
monthly instalments. Calculate each instalment, in dollars.

**Answer:** 350

**Working:** Deposit $= 0.25 \times 4800 = 1200$.

The deposit is part of the \$5 400, so the instalments cover
$5400 - 1200 = 4200$. Each instalment: $4200 \div 12 = 350$.

### 2.5 | mcq | 1 mark | difficulty 2
A mortgage of \$250 000 is repaid at \$1 800 per month for 25 years. What is the
total amount repaid?

- A. \$45 000
- B. \$450 000
- C. \$540 000
- D. \$790 000

**Answer:** C

**Working:** Number of payments: $25 \times 12 = 300$.
Total: $300 \times 1800 = 540\,000$.

A forgets the months ($25 \times 1800$). D adds the loan onto the repayments,
but the repayments already include it.

### 2.5 | structured | 6 marks | difficulty 3
A used car has a cash price of \$48 000. It can also be bought on hire purchase
with a deposit of 15% of the cash price and 36 monthly instalments of \$1 450.

(a) Calculate the deposit. **(1 mark)**

(b) Calculate the hire purchase price. **(2 marks)**

(c) How much more than the cash price does the car cost on hire purchase?
**(1 mark)**

(d) Express this extra cost as a percentage of the cash price. **(2 marks)**

**Answer:** (a) \$7200 (b) \$59400 (c) \$11400 (d) 23.75%

**Working:** (a) $0.15 \times 48\,000 = 7200$.

(b) Instalments: $36 \times 1450 = 52\,200$ (1 mark).
HP price: $7200 + 52\,200 = 59\,400$ (1 mark).

(c) $59\,400 - 48\,000 = 11\,400$.

(d) $\frac{11\,400}{48\,000} \times 100\% = 23.75\%$ (1 mark for the fraction, 1
for the answer).
