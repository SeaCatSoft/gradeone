## Lesson

### Utility bills

Electricity and water are charged by how much you use. A **meter** keeps a
running total. To find what you used in a billing period, subtract:

$$\text{units used} = \text{present reading} - \text{previous reading}$$

Electricity is measured in **kilowatt-hours** (kWh), often just called
"units". Water is often measured in cubic metres or gallons.

Most bills also have a **fixed charge** you pay whatever you use, and the rate
per unit may change once you pass a certain amount.

> An electricity company charges a fixed \$15 a month, plus \$0.40 per kWh for
> the first 100 kWh and \$0.55 per kWh after that. The meter read 34 512 last
> month and 34 838 this month.

- Units used: $34\,838 - 34\,512 = 326$ kWh
- First 100 kWh: $100 \times 0.40 = \$40.00$
- Remaining $326 - 100 = 226$ kWh: $226 \times 0.55 = \$124.30$
- Fixed charge: \$15.00
- **Total:** $40 + 124.30 + 15 = \$179.30$

The common slip is charging **all** 326 units at the higher rate. Only the units
**above** 100 cost \$0.55.

### Invoices and shopping bills

An **invoice** lists what was bought. For each line:

$$\text{line total} = \text{quantity} \times \text{unit price}$$

Add the line totals for the **subtotal**, then add any tax.

| Item | Quantity | Unit price | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| Exercise books | 12 | \$4.50 | \$54.00 |
| Pens | 20 | \$1.25 | \$25.00 |
| Geometry sets | 3 | \$18.00 | \$54.00 |
| | | **Subtotal** | **\$133.00** |
| | | **Tax (10%)** | **\$13.30** |
| | | **Total** | **\$146.30** |

When a question gives a bill with gaps, fill them the same way — sometimes
working backwards, dividing a total by the quantity to find the unit price.

### Measures and money: the best buy

Which is better value: rice at 2 kg for \$9.60, or 5 kg for \$23.00?

Compare the price of the **same amount** — a **unit price**:

| Pack | Price per kg |
|---|---|
| 2 kg for \$9.60 | $9.60 \div 2 = \$4.80$ |
| 5 kg for \$23.00 | $23.00 \div 5 = \$4.60$ |

The 5 kg bag is cheaper per kilogram, so it's the better buy.

Convert units first if the packs are measured differently. To compare 750 g
with 2 kg, use grams for both, or kilograms for both.

You can also compare by asking how much you get **per dollar** — the bigger
amount per dollar is the better buy. Either method works; just use the same
one for every option.

## Flashcards

### 2.10 | How do you find the units used from two meter readings?
Subtract: present reading minus previous reading.

### 2.10 | With a tiered rate, which units are charged at the higher rate?
Only the units **above** the threshold. The first block is still charged at
the lower rate.

### 2.10 | How is each line of an invoice calculated?
Quantity multiplied by unit price.

### 2.9 | How do you decide which pack is the best buy?
Find the price of the **same amount** for each (a unit price, like price per
kg), after converting to the same units. The lowest unit price is the best
buy.

### 2.9 | Which is better value: 400 g for \$5 or 1 kg for \$11?
1 kg for \$11. Per 100 g: $5 \div 4 = \$1.25$ against $11 \div 10 = \$1.10$.

## Questions

### 2.10 | numeric | 1 mark | difficulty 2
A water meter reading changed from 2 485 to 2 710 units. Water costs \$0.48 per
unit, plus a fixed charge of \$12. Calculate the bill, in dollars.

**Answer:** 120

**Working:** Units used: $2710 - 2485 = 225$.

Cost: $225 \times 0.48 + 12 = 108 + 12 = 120$.

### 2.9 | mcq | 1 mark | difficulty 2
Which bottle of juice is the best value?

- A. 500 mL for \$4.00
- B. 1 L for \$7.50
- C. 1.5 L for \$11.70
- D. 2 L for \$15.80

**Answer:** B

**Working:** Price per litre: A $4.00 \div 0.5 = \$8.00$; B \$7.50;
C $11.70 \div 1.5 = \$7.80$; D $15.80 \div 2 = \$7.90$.

B has the lowest price per litre. The biggest bottle is not always the best
value.

### 2.10 | numeric | 1 mark | difficulty 3
Electricity costs \$0.35 per kWh for the first 150 kWh and \$0.50 per kWh for
every kWh after that. A household uses 280 kWh. Calculate the cost, in
dollars. (There is no fixed charge.)

**Answer:** 117.5

**Working:** First 150 kWh: $150 \times 0.35 = 52.50$.

Remaining $280 - 150 = 130$ kWh: $130 \times 0.50 = 65.00$.

Total: $52.50 + 65.00 = 117.50$.

### 2.10 | structured | 5 marks | difficulty 3
A café's invoice is shown, with some entries missing.

| Item | Quantity | Unit price | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bags of flour | 8 | \$22.50 | $P$ |
| Trays of eggs | $Q$ | \$36.00 | \$252.00 |
| Blocks of butter | 15 | $R$ | \$97.50 |
| | | **Subtotal** | $S$ |

(a) Find the values of $P$, $Q$ and $R$. **(3 marks)**

(b) Find the subtotal, $S$. **(1 mark)**

(c) Sales tax of 12% is added to the subtotal. Find the amount due.
**(1 mark)**

**Answer:** (a) $P = \$180.00$, $Q = 7$, $R = \$6.50$ (b) \$529.50 (c) \$593.04

**Working:** (a) $P = 8 \times 22.50 = 180.00$.
$Q = 252 \div 36 = 7$.
$R = 97.50 \div 15 = 6.50$. (1 mark each.)

(b) $S = 180 + 252 + 97.50 = 529.50$.

(c) $1.12 \times 529.50 = 593.04$.
