## Lesson

### Wages and salaries

A **wage** is paid by the hour (or day or week). A **salary** is a fixed
amount per year, usually paid monthly.

$$\text{Basic wage} = \text{hours worked} \times \text{hourly rate}$$

Hours beyond the normal working week are **overtime**, usually paid at a
higher rate. "Time and a half" means $1.5 \times$ the normal rate; "double
time" means $2 \times$.

> Andre earns \$18 an hour for a 40-hour week, and time and a half for
> overtime. One week he works 46 hours.

- Basic pay: $40 \times 18 = \$720$
- Overtime rate: $1.5 \times 18 = \$27$ an hour
- Overtime pay: $6 \times 27 = \$162$
- **Total:** $720 + 162 = \$882$

Some jobs pay **commission**: a percentage of the value of what you sell.

> A salesperson earns a basic \$2 000 a month plus 5% commission on sales.
> Sales this month were \$36 000.

Commission: $0.05 \times 36\,000 = \$1800$. Total pay: $2000 + 1800 = \$3800$.

### Income tax

Income tax is not charged on every dollar you earn. Most countries give a
**tax-free allowance**, and tax is charged only on the rest — the **taxable
income**.

$$\text{Taxable income} = \text{gross income} - \text{allowances}$$

> Nadia earns \$96 000 a year. Her tax-free allowance is \$36 000, and tax is
> charged at 25% on taxable income.

- Taxable income: $96\,000 - 36\,000 = \$60\,000$
- Income tax: $0.25 \times 60\,000 = \$15\,000$
- Income after tax: $96\,000 - 15\,000 = \$81\,000$

Charging 25% on the whole \$96 000 is the classic mistake. Take the allowance
off **first**.

### Property rates

Local councils charge **rates** (a property tax) on land and buildings. Each
property has an assessed value, and the rate is a percentage of that value —
sometimes written as a number of cents per dollar.

> A house has an assessed value of \$300 000. Rates are charged at 0.5% a year.

$$\text{Rates} = 0.005 \times 300\,000 = \$1500 \text{ a year}$$

### Insurance

An insurance **premium** is what you pay for cover. It's often quoted per
\$100 (or per \$1 000) of the value insured.

> A house is insured for \$400 000. The premium is \$1.20 per \$100 insured.

The house is worth $400\,000 \div 100 = 4000$ lots of \$100, so

$$\text{Premium} = 4000 \times 1.20 = \$4800 \text{ a year}$$

### Investments

Money that is invested earns a **return** — interest from a bank, or a
dividend from shares. The return is often compared as a percentage of the
amount invested, just like percentage profit:

$$\text{Percentage return} = \frac{\text{return}}{\text{amount invested}} \times 100\%$$

An investment of \$5 000 that pays \$200 a year returns
$\frac{200}{5000} \times 100\% = 4\%$ a year.

## Flashcards

### 2.10 | What does "time and a half" mean?
Overtime is paid at $1.5 \times$ the normal hourly rate.

### 2.10 | What is taxable income?
Gross income minus tax-free allowances. Tax is charged only on this part.

### 2.10 | What is commission?
Pay worked out as a percentage of the value of the sales made.

### 2.10 | The premium is \$2 per \$100 insured. What is the premium on \$50 000?
\$1000. $50\,000 \div 100 = 500$, and $500 \times 2 = 1000$.

### 2.10 | Gross income \$50 000, allowance \$20 000, tax 20%. How much tax?
\$6 000. Taxable income is \$30 000, and 20% of that is \$6 000.

## Questions

### 2.10 | numeric | 1 mark | difficulty 2
Keron is paid \$15 an hour for a 40-hour week and double time for overtime. He
works 44 hours. Calculate his pay for the week, in dollars.

**Answer:** 720

**Working:** Basic: $40 \times 15 = 600$. Overtime rate: $2 \times 15 = 30$.
Overtime pay: $4 \times 30 = 120$. Total: $600 + 120 = 720$.

### 2.10 | mcq | 1 mark | difficulty 2
A man earns \$84 000 a year. The first \$30 000 is tax-free, and the rest is
taxed at 20%. How much income tax does he pay?

- A. \$6 000
- B. \$10 800
- C. \$16 800
- D. \$22 800

**Answer:** B

**Working:** Taxable income: $84\,000 - 30\,000 = 54\,000$.
Tax: $0.20 \times 54\,000 = 10\,800$.

C taxes the whole \$84 000. A taxes only the allowance.

### 2.10 | numeric | 1 mark | difficulty 2
A car worth \$65 000 is insured at a premium of \$3.50 per \$100 of its value.
Calculate the annual premium, in dollars.

**Answer:** 2275

**Working:** $65\,000 \div 100 = 650$. Premium: $650 \times 3.50 = 2275$.

### 2.10 | structured | 6 marks | difficulty 3
Mrs Baptiste's gross monthly salary is \$7 500.

(a) Calculate her gross annual salary. **(1 mark)**

(b) Her annual tax-free allowance is \$25 000. Income tax is charged at 25% on
the rest. Calculate her annual income tax. **(3 marks)**

(c) She also pays 5% of her **gross** salary into a pension fund. Calculate
her monthly take-home pay after tax and pension, assuming tax is spread
evenly over the 12 months. **(2 marks)**

**Answer:** (a) \$90000 (b) \$16250 (c) \$5770.83

**Working:** (a) $12 \times 7500 = 90\,000$.

(b) Taxable income: $90\,000 - 25\,000 = 65\,000$ (1 mark).
Tax: $0.25 \times 65\,000 = 16\,250$ (2 marks — 1 for the method, 1 for the
answer).

(c) Monthly tax: $16\,250 \div 12 = 1354.1\overline{6}$.
Monthly pension: $0.05 \times 7500 = 375$ (1 mark).
Take-home: $7500 - 1354.17 - 375 = 5770.83$ (1 mark).
