## Lesson

When you save money in a bank, the bank pays you for the use of it. When you
borrow, you pay the lender. Either way, that payment is **interest**.

### The vocabulary

| Term | Meaning | Symbol |
|---|---|---|
| Principal | the amount saved or borrowed | $P$ |
| Rate | the percentage charged or paid **per year** | $R$ |
| Time | how long, **in years** | $T$ |
| Interest | the extra money paid | $I$ |
| Amount | principal plus interest | $A$ |

### The formula

With **simple interest**, the interest is the same every year, because it's
always worked out on the original principal.

$$I = \frac{P \times R \times T}{100}$$

$$A = P + I$$

> Find the simple interest on \$2 500 at 4% per year for 3 years.

$$I = \frac{2500 \times 4 \times 3}{100} = \frac{30\,000}{100} = \$300$$

The amount at the end is $2500 + 300 = \$2800$.

Each year earns $\frac{2500 \times 4}{100} = \$100$; three years earns \$300.
The formula is just that, done in one go.

### Time must be in years

The rate is **per year**, so $T$ must be in years too.

| Given | Use |
|---|---|
| 6 months | $T = \frac{6}{12} = 0.5$ |
| 18 months | $T = \frac{18}{12} = 1.5$ |
| 9 months | $T = \frac{9}{12} = 0.75$ |

Putting $T = 6$ for six months makes the interest twelve times too big.

### Finding $P$, $R$ or $T$

Rearrange the formula to make the unknown the subject:

$$P = \frac{100I}{RT} \qquad R = \frac{100I}{PT} \qquad T = \frac{100I}{PR}$$

> What rate of simple interest makes \$800 earn \$120 in 3 years?

$$R = \frac{100 \times 120}{800 \times 3} = \frac{12\,000}{2400} = 5\%$$

If you're given the **amount** rather than the interest, subtract first.
\$800 growing to \$920 means $I = 920 - 800 = \$120$.

## Flashcards

### 2.6 | State the simple interest formula.
$$I = \frac{PRT}{100}$$

$P$ principal, $R$ rate per year (%), $T$ time in years.

### 2.6 | What is the difference between the interest and the amount?
The **interest** is the extra money. The **amount** is principal plus
interest: $A = P + I$.

### 2.6 | What value of $T$ do you use for 8 months?
$T = \frac{8}{12} = \frac{2}{3}$ years.

### 2.6 | Rearrange $I = \frac{PRT}{100}$ to find $R$.
$$R = \frac{100I}{PT}$$

### 2.6 | Why is simple interest the same every year?
It is always calculated on the **original principal**, which never changes.

## Questions

### 2.6 | numeric | 1 mark | difficulty 1
Calculate the simple interest on \$1 200 at 5% per annum for 2 years.

**Answer:** 120

**Working:** $I = \dfrac{1200 \times 5 \times 2}{100} = 120$.

### 2.6 | numeric | 1 mark | difficulty 2
Calculate the simple interest on \$3 000 at 6% per annum for 9 months.

**Answer:** 135

**Working:** Time in years: $T = \frac{9}{12} = 0.75$.

$I = \dfrac{3000 \times 6 \times 0.75}{100} = 135$.

### 2.6 | mcq | 1 mark | difficulty 2
\$5 000 is invested at 3.5% simple interest per year. What is the amount after
4 years?

- A. \$700
- B. \$5 175
- C. \$5 700
- D. \$5 875

**Answer:** C

**Working:** $I = \dfrac{5000 \times 3.5 \times 4}{100} = 700$, so
$A = 5000 + 700 = 5700$.

A is the interest alone. B is one year's interest added.

### 2.6 | numeric | 1 mark | difficulty 3
How many years will it take for \$2 000 to earn \$360 simple interest at 4.5%
per annum?

**Answer:** 4

**Working:** $T = \dfrac{100I}{PR} = \dfrac{100 \times 360}{2000 \times 4.5} = \dfrac{36\,000}{9000} = 4$ years.

### 2.6 | structured | 5 marks | difficulty 3
Maria borrows \$6 400 from a credit union at a simple interest rate of 7.5%
per annum. She repays the loan and interest in full after 30 months.

(a) Express 30 months in years. **(1 mark)**

(b) Calculate the interest she pays. **(2 marks)**

(c) She repays in 30 equal monthly instalments. How much is each instalment?
**(2 marks)**

**Answer:** (a) 2.5 years (b) \$1200 (c) \$253.33

**Working:** (a) $30 \div 12 = 2.5$ years.

(b) $I = \dfrac{6400 \times 7.5 \times 2.5}{100} = 1200$. (1 mark for
substitution, 1 for the answer.)

(c) Total to repay $= 6400 + 1200 = 7600$ (1 mark).
$7600 \div 30 = 253.333\ldots$, so \$253.33 per month (1 mark).
