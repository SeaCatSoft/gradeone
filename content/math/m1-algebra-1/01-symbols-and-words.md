## Lesson

Algebra is a shorthand. Instead of writing "a number, multiplied by three,
then increased by five", we write $3n + 5$. The letter stands for a number we
don't know yet, or one that can change — a **variable**.

### The conventions

Algebra drops some symbols to save space:

| Written | Means |
|---|---|
| $3n$ | $3 \times n$ (the number goes in front) |
| $ab$ | $a \times b$ |
| $n^2$ | $n \times n$ |
| $\frac{x}{4}$ | $x \div 4$ |
| $5(x + 2)$ | $5 \times (x + 2)$ |

So $4ab^2$ means $4 \times a \times b \times b$. Note the square applies only to
$b$, not to $4ab$.

Some vocabulary:

- A **term** is a single number, letter, or product of them: $7$, $x$, $3xy$.
- An **expression** is terms joined by $+$ or $-$: $3x + 2y - 5$.
- The number in front of a letter is its **coefficient**: in $-6y$, it is $-6$.
- An **equation** says two things are equal: $3x + 2 = 11$.

### Symbols for relations

| Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|
| $=$ | is equal to |
| $\neq$ | is not equal to |
| $<$, $>$ | is less than, is greater than |
| $\leq$, $\geq$ | is less than or equal to, is greater than or equal to |

### From words to symbols

Look for the key words, and take care with **order**.

| Words | Symbols |
|---|---|
| 7 more than $x$ | $x + 7$ |
| 7 less than $x$ | $x - 7$ |
| $x$ less than 7 | $7 - x$ |
| the product of $x$ and $y$ | $xy$ |
| $x$ divided by 3 | $\frac{x}{3}$ |
| twice $x$, then add 5 | $2x + 5$ |
| twice the sum of $x$ and 5 | $2(x + 5)$ |
| the square of $x$ | $x^2$ |

Two to watch:

- "7 less than $x$" is $x - 7$, **not** $7 - x$. Start with $x$ and take 7 away.
- "twice $x$ plus 5" and "twice the **sum** of $x$ and 5" are different. "The
  sum" means add first, so you need brackets.

### From symbols to words

Read it back in the order it's calculated. $\frac{n - 4}{2}$ is "4 subtracted
from $n$, then the result halved" — or "half of 4 less than $n$".

### Writing an expression for a situation

> Pencils cost $p$ cents each and erasers cost $e$ cents each. Write an
> expression for the cost of 5 pencils and 3 erasers.

$$5p + 3e \text{ cents}$$

Define what each letter stands for, and include units.

## Flashcards

### 5.1 | What does $5ab$ mean?
$5 \times a \times b$.

### 5.1 | In $4x^2$, what is squared?
Only $x$. $4x^2 = 4 \times x \times x$. For $(4x)^2$ the whole thing is squared.

### 5.2 | Write "8 less than $y$" in symbols.
$y - 8$.

> Hint: start with $y$, then take 8 away.

### 5.2 | Write "three times the sum of $a$ and $b$" in symbols.
$3(a + b)$. "The sum" needs brackets.

### 5.1 | What is the coefficient of $y$ in $7 - 4y$?
$-4$. The sign belongs to the coefficient.

## Questions

### 5.2 | mcq | 1 mark | difficulty 1
"Five more than twice a number $n$" is written as

- A. $5n + 2$
- B. $2n + 5$
- C. $2(n + 5)$
- D. $7n$

**Answer:** B

**Working:** Twice the number is $2n$; five more is $+ 5$. So $2n + 5$.

C would be "twice the sum of $n$ and 5".

### 5.2 | mcq | 1 mark | difficulty 2
Which expression means "the square of the difference between $x$ and 3"?

- A. $x^2 - 3$
- B. $x^2 - 9$
- C. $(x - 3)^2$
- D. $x - 3^2$

**Answer:** C

**Working:** "The difference between $x$ and 3" is $x - 3$, and then that whole
difference is squared: $(x - 3)^2$.

A squares only $x$. D squares only the 3.

### 5.2 | mcq | 1 mark | difficulty 2
A taxi charges a fixed fee of \$8 plus \$3 per kilometre. Which expression gives
the cost, in dollars, of a trip of $k$ kilometres?

- A. $8k + 3$
- B. $11k$
- C. $8 + 3k$
- D. $3(8 + k)$

**Answer:** C

**Working:** The \$8 is paid once. The \$3 is paid for each of the $k$ km:
$3k$. Total: $8 + 3k$.

### 5.1 | mcq | 1 mark | difficulty 2
"$x$ is at least 18" is written as

- A. $x < 18$
- B. $x > 18$
- C. $x \leq 18$
- D. $x \geq 18$

**Answer:** D

**Working:** "At least 18" means 18 or more, so $x \geq 18$.

### 5.2 | structured | 4 marks | difficulty 2
Mia is $m$ years old. Her brother is 4 years older than her, and her mother is
three times as old as Mia.

(a) Write an expression for her brother's age. **(1 mark)**

(b) Write an expression for her mother's age. **(1 mark)**

(c) Write an expression for the sum of all three ages, in its simplest form.
**(2 marks)**

**Answer:** (a) $m + 4$ (b) $3m$ (c) $5m + 4$

**Working:** (c) $m + (m + 4) + 3m = 5m + 4$. (1 mark for the unsimplified
sum, 1 for collecting like terms.)
