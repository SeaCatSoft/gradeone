## Lesson

An **equation** says two expressions are equal. **Solving** it means finding
the value of the unknown that makes it true.

### The balance

Think of an equation as a balanced scale. Whatever you do to one side, you
must do to the other, and it stays balanced. The aim is to get the unknown
**alone** on one side.

Undo operations in reverse order, using the **inverse** of each:
$+$ undoes $-$, $\times$ undoes $\div$, and vice versa.

$$
\begin{aligned}
3x + 7 &= 22 \\
3x &= 15 &&\text{subtract 7 from both sides} \\
x &= 5 &&\text{divide both sides by 3}
\end{aligned}
$$

**Check** by substituting: $3(5) + 7 = 22$. ✓ It takes seconds and catches most
errors.

### Unknowns on both sides

Collect the unknowns on one side (usually whichever has more of them) and the
numbers on the other.

$$
\begin{aligned}
5x - 4 &= 2x + 11 \\
3x - 4 &= 11 &&\text{subtract } 2x \\
3x &= 15 &&\text{add 4} \\
x &= 5
\end{aligned}
$$

### Brackets

Expand first, then solve.

$$
\begin{aligned}
4(x - 3) &= 2(x + 5) \\
4x - 12 &= 2x + 10 \\
2x &= 22 \\
x &= 11
\end{aligned}
$$

### Fractions

Multiply **every term** on both sides by the LCM of the denominators. That
clears the fractions in one step.

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{x}{3} + \frac{x}{4} &= 7 \\
4x + 3x &= 84 &&\text{multiply every term by 12} \\
7x &= 84 \\
x &= 12
\end{aligned}
$$

With a bracketed top, keep the bracket while multiplying:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{x + 2}{5} &= \frac{x - 1}{2} \\
2(x + 2) &= 5(x - 1) &&\text{multiply both sides by 10} \\
2x + 4 &= 5x - 5 \\
9 &= 3x \\
x &= 3
\end{aligned}
$$

Answers don't have to be whole numbers. $4x = 10$ gives $x = 2.5$ (or
$\frac{5}{2}$), and a negative answer is perfectly fine.

## Flashcards

### 5.10 | What is the golden rule for solving equations?
Whatever you do to one side, do to the other.

### 5.10 | Solve $2x - 5 = 13$.
$x = 9$. Add 5: $2x = 18$. Divide by 2.

### 5.10 | Solve $7x + 2 = 3x + 18$.
$x = 4$. Subtract $3x$: $4x + 2 = 18$, so $4x = 16$.

### 5.10 | How do you clear fractions from an equation?
Multiply **every term** on both sides by the LCM of the denominators.

### 5.10 | How do you check a solution?
Substitute it back into the original equation and see if both sides are equal.

## Questions

### 5.10 | numeric | 1 mark | difficulty 1
Solve $4x + 9 = 37$.

**Answer:** 7

**Working:** $4x = 28$, so $x = 7$.

### 5.10 | numeric | 1 mark | difficulty 2
Solve $3(2x - 1) = 21$.

**Answer:** 4

**Working:** $6x - 3 = 21$, so $6x = 24$ and $x = 4$.

### 5.10 | numeric | 1 mark | difficulty 2
Solve $8 - 3x = 2x - 12$.

**Answer:** 4

**Working:** Add $3x$ to both sides: $8 = 5x - 12$. Add 12: $20 = 5x$. So $x = 4$.

Check: $8 - 12 = -4$ and $8 - 12 = -4$. ✓

### 5.10 | numeric | 1 mark | difficulty 3
Solve $\dfrac{x}{2} - \dfrac{x}{5} = 6$.

**Answer:** 20

**Working:** Multiply every term by 10: $5x - 2x = 60$, so $3x = 60$ and $x = 20$.

### 5.10 | structured | 4 marks | difficulty 3
Solve the equation $\dfrac{2x - 1}{3} = \dfrac{x + 4}{2}$, showing each step.

**Answer:** $x = 14$

**Working:** Multiply both sides by 6: $2(2x - 1) = 3(x + 4)$ (1 mark).

Expand: $4x - 2 = 3x + 12$ (1 mark).

Collect: $4x - 3x = 12 + 2$ (1 mark), so $x = 14$ (1 mark).

Check: $\dfrac{27}{3} = 9$ and $\dfrac{18}{2} = 9$. ✓
