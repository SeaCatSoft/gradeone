## Lesson

**Directed numbers** have a sign that shows direction: positive numbers lie to
the right of zero on a number line, negative numbers to the left. They
describe temperatures below freezing, money owed, depths below sea level.

### Adding and subtracting

Think of a number line. **Adding a positive** moves right; **subtracting a
positive** moves left.

- $-3 + 5 = 2$ (start at $-3$, move 5 right)
- $2 - 7 = -5$ (start at 2, move 7 left)
- $-4 - 3 = -7$ (start at $-4$, move 3 more left)

With a negative number being added or subtracted, first combine the two signs
next to each other:

| Signs | Become | Example |
|---|---|---|
| $+\,(+)$ | $+$ | $5 + (+2) = 5 + 2 = 7$ |
| $+\,(-)$ | $-$ | $5 + (-2) = 5 - 2 = 3$ |
| $-\,(+)$ | $-$ | $5 - (+2) = 5 - 2 = 3$ |
| $-\,(-)$ | $+$ | $5 - (-2) = 5 + 2 = 7$ |

**Same signs make plus; different signs make minus.**

Subtracting a negative is the one that feels odd. Think of it as taking away a
debt: if someone cancels a \$2 debt you owe, you're \$2 better off.

### Multiplying and dividing

For multiplication and division, look at the signs of the two numbers:

| Signs | Answer | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| same | **positive** | $(-4) \times (-3) = 12$, $\;(-20) \div (-5) = 4$ |
| different | **negative** | $(-4) \times 3 = -12$, $\;20 \div (-5) = -4$ |

For several numbers multiplied together, count the negatives. An **even**
number of negatives gives a positive answer; an **odd** number gives a
negative one.

$$(-2) \times (-3) \times (-1) = -6 \quad \text{(three negatives: negative)}$$

### Powers of negative numbers

$(-3)^2 = (-3) \times (-3) = 9$, but $(-3)^3 = -27$. Even powers of a negative
number are positive; odd powers are negative.

And remember the bracket: $-3^2 = -9$, because only the 3 is squared.

### Order of operations still applies

$$-2 \times (5 - 8) + (-6) \div 3 = -2 \times (-3) + (-2) = 6 - 2 = 4$$

Work out each part carefully, one sign at a time.

## Flashcards

### 5.3 | What is $-7 + 10$?
$3$. Start at $-7$ and move 10 to the right.

### 5.3 | What is $4 - (-6)$?
$10$. Two minus signs together make plus: $4 + 6$.

### 5.3 | What is the sign of the product of two negative numbers?
**Positive.** $(-5) \times (-2) = 10$.

### 5.3 | What is $(-24) \div 6$?
$-4$. Different signs give a negative answer.

### 5.3 | Is $(-2)^5$ positive or negative?
**Negative**: $-32$. An odd power of a negative number is negative.

## Questions

### 5.3 | numeric | 1 mark | difficulty 1
Evaluate $-8 + 3$.

**Answer:** -5

**Working:** Start at $-8$ and move 3 right: $-5$.

### 5.3 | numeric | 1 mark | difficulty 2
Evaluate $-5 - (-9)$.

**Answer:** 4

**Working:** $-(-9) = +9$, so $-5 + 9 = 4$.

### 5.3 | mcq | 1 mark | difficulty 2
$(-6) \times (-4) \div (-3) =$

- A. $-8$
- B. $8$
- C. $-2$
- D. $72$

**Answer:** A

**Working:** $(-6) \times (-4) = 24$, then $24 \div (-3) = -8$.

Three negative signs, an odd number, so the answer is negative.

### 5.3 | numeric | 1 mark | difficulty 3
The temperature at midnight was $-4\,°\text{C}$. By noon it had risen by
$11\,°\text{C}$, and by the next midnight it had fallen by $9\,°\text{C}$. What was
the temperature at the second midnight, in °C?

**Answer:** -2

**Working:** $-4 + 11 = 7$, then $7 - 9 = -2$ °C.

### 5.3 | structured | 3 marks | difficulty 3
Given that $a = -3$, $b = 2$ and $c = -1$, evaluate

(a) $a - b + c$ **(1 mark)**

(b) $a^2 + bc$ **(1 mark)**

(c) $\dfrac{ab}{c}$ **(1 mark)**

**Answer:** (a) $-6$ (b) $7$ (c) $6$

**Working:** (a) $-3 - 2 + (-1) = -6$.

(b) $(-3)^2 + 2 \times (-1) = 9 - 2 = 7$.

(c) $\dfrac{(-3)(2)}{-1} = \dfrac{-6}{-1} = 6$.
