## Lesson

### Like terms

**Like terms** have exactly the same letters, raised to the same powers. Only
the numbers in front can differ.

| Like terms | Not like terms |
|---|---|
| $3x$ and $-5x$ | $3x$ and $3x^2$ |
| $2ab$ and $7ba$ | $2ab$ and $2a$ |
| $x^2y$ and $4x^2y$ | $x^2y$ and $xy^2$ |

($ab$ and $ba$ are alike, because $a \times b = b \times a$.)

### Adding and subtracting: collect like terms

Only like terms can be added or subtracted. Combine their coefficients, and
keep each term's sign attached to it:

$$5x + 3y - 2x + 4y - y = (5 - 2)x + (3 + 4 - 1)y = 3x + 6y$$

$3x + 6y$ can't be simplified further: $x$ and $y$ are different, just as
3 apples and 6 oranges don't make 9 of anything.

A neat habit: underline or colour each group of like terms, **including the
sign in front**, before collecting.

### Multiplying

Multiply the numbers, then the letters. Unlike adding, **any** terms can be
multiplied:

$$3a \times 4b = 12ab \qquad 2x \times 5x = 10x^2 \qquad -3p \times 2pq = -6p^2q$$

Letters are usually written in alphabetical order.

### Dividing

Write it as a fraction and cancel common factors — numbers and letters:

$$\frac{12x^2y}{4xy} = \frac{12}{4} \times \frac{x^2}{x} \times \frac{y}{y} = 3x$$

### Substitution

To **substitute** is to replace each letter with its value, then calculate.
Put the value in **brackets** — it prevents sign errors with negatives.

> Find the value of $2x^2 - 3y$ when $x = -4$ and $y = 5$.

$$2(-4)^2 - 3(5) = 2(16) - 15 = 32 - 15 = 17$$

Without brackets, $2 \times -4^2$ tempts you to square only the 4, or to square
$2 \times -4$. The bracket makes it clear that $-4$ is what gets squared.

Follow BODMAS after substituting: powers before multiplying.

## Flashcards

### 5.4 | What are like terms?
Terms with exactly the same letters raised to the same powers, such as $4xy$
and $-xy$.

### 5.4 | Simplify $7a - 2b - 3a + 5b$.
$4a + 3b$.

### 5.4 | Simplify $3x \times 4x$.
$12x^2$. Multiply the numbers, then $x \times x = x^2$.

### 5.4 | Can $5x + 2x^2$ be simplified?
No. $x$ and $x^2$ are not like terms.

### 5.6 | Why put substituted values in brackets?
To keep signs and powers right, especially with negatives: $(-3)^2 = 9$.

## Questions

### 5.4 | mcq | 1 mark | difficulty 1
Simplify $6m + 2n - m + 3n$.

- A. $5m + 5n$
- B. $7m + 5n$
- C. $10mn$
- D. $6m + 5n$

**Answer:** A

**Working:** $6m - m = 5m$ and $2n + 3n = 5n$, giving $5m + 5n$.

B adds the $m$ instead of subtracting it. D forgets it altogether.

### 5.4 | mcq | 1 mark | difficulty 2
$\dfrac{18a^3b}{6ab}$ simplifies to

- A. $3a^2$
- B. $3a^3$
- C. $12a^2$
- D. $3a^2b$

**Answer:** A

**Working:** $18 \div 6 = 3$; $a^3 \div a = a^2$; $b \div b = 1$. So $3a^2$.

### 5.6 | numeric | 1 mark | difficulty 2
Find the value of $5p - 2q$ when $p = 3$ and $q = -4$.

**Answer:** 23

**Working:** $5(3) - 2(-4) = 15 + 8 = 23$.

### 5.6 | numeric | 1 mark | difficulty 3
Given $x = -2$ and $y = 3$, evaluate $x^3 + 2xy - y^2$.

**Answer:** -29

**Working:** $(-2)^3 + 2(-2)(3) - (3)^2 = -8 - 12 - 9 = -29$.

### 5.4 | structured | 4 marks | difficulty 3
(a) Simplify $4x^2 + 3x - 5 - x^2 + 2x + 1$. **(2 marks)**

(b) Hence find the value of the expression when $x = -3$. **(2 marks)**

**Answer:** (a) $3x^2 + 5x - 4$ (b) $8$

**Working:** (a) $x^2$ terms: $4x^2 - x^2 = 3x^2$. $x$ terms: $3x + 2x = 5x$.
Numbers: $-5 + 1 = -4$. (1 mark for two groups right, 2 for all three.)

(b) $3(-3)^2 + 5(-3) - 4 = 27 - 15 - 4 = 8$.
