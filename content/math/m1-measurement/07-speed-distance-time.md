## Lesson

### The three formulas

Speed tells you how much distance is covered in each unit of time:

$$\text{speed} = \frac{\text{distance}}{\text{time}}$$

Rearranged:

$$\text{distance} = \text{speed} \times \text{time} \qquad \text{time} = \frac{\text{distance}}{\text{speed}}$$

A quick way to remember all three is the triangle with $D$ on top and $S$ and
$T$ underneath. Cover the one you want: $D$ leaves $S \times T$; $S$ leaves
$\frac{D}{T}$; $T$ leaves $\frac{D}{S}$.

### Keep the units matched

If speed is in km/h, the distance must be in km and the time in **hours**.

Time given in minutes? Change to hours first — and remember time isn't
decimal. 45 minutes is $\frac{45}{60} = 0.75$ h; 1 h 20 min is
$1\frac{20}{60} = 1\frac{1}{3}$ h.

> A car travels 150 km in 2 hours 30 minutes. What is its average speed?

$2 \text{ h } 30 \text{ min} = 2.5 \text{ h}$, so speed $= \frac{150}{2.5} = 60$ km/h.

Using $2.30$ for the time would give about 65.2 km/h — wrong, because 30
minutes is half an hour, not 0.3 of one.

### Converting between km/h and m/s

$$1 \text{ km/h} = \frac{1000 \text{ m}}{3600 \text{ s}} = \frac{5}{18} \text{ m/s}$$

- km/h → m/s: multiply by $\frac{5}{18}$ (or divide by 3.6)
- m/s → km/h: multiply by $\frac{18}{5}$ (or multiply by 3.6)

So $72 \text{ km/h} = 72 \times \frac{5}{18} = 20$ m/s.

### Average speed for a whole journey

$$\text{average speed} = \frac{\text{total distance}}{\text{total time}}$$

This is **not** the average of the speeds.

> A cyclist rides 60 km at 30 km/h, then another 60 km at 60 km/h.

- First part: $60 \div 30 = 2$ h. Second part: $60 \div 60 = 1$ h.
- Total: 120 km in 3 h, so the average speed is $\frac{120}{3} = 40$ km/h.

Averaging the two speeds gives 45 km/h, which is wrong. The cyclist spends
longer at the slower speed, so it counts for more.

## Flashcards

### 4.10 | State the formula for speed.
$$\text{speed} = \frac{\text{distance}}{\text{time}}$$

### 4.10 | How do you find the time taken, given distance and speed?
$$\text{time} = \frac{\text{distance}}{\text{speed}}$$

### 4.10 | Write 1 hour 15 minutes as a decimal of an hour.
$1.25$ h. 15 minutes is $\frac{15}{60} = 0.25$ h.

### 4.10 | Convert 90 km/h to m/s.
$90 \times \frac{5}{18} = 25$ m/s.

### 4.10 | How do you find the average speed for a whole journey?
Total distance ÷ total time. **Not** the average of the separate speeds.

## Questions

### 4.10 | numeric | 1 mark | difficulty 1
A bus travels 210 km in 3 hours. Find its average speed, in km/h.

**Answer:** 70

**Working:** $210 \div 3 = 70$ km/h.

### 4.10 | numeric | 1 mark | difficulty 2
A car travels at 64 km/h for 45 minutes. How far does it go, in km?

**Answer:** 48

**Working:** 45 minutes $= 0.75$ h. Distance $= 64 \times 0.75 = 48$ km.

### 4.10 | mcq | 1 mark | difficulty 2
A sprinter runs at 10 m/s. What is this speed in km/h?

- A. 2.8 km/h
- B. 10 km/h
- C. 36 km/h
- D. 600 km/h

**Answer:** C

**Working:** m/s to km/h: multiply by 3.6. $10 \times 3.6 = 36$ km/h.

A divides instead of multiplying. D multiplies by 60 only, converting seconds
to minutes but not metres to kilometres.

### 4.10 | mcq | 1 mark | difficulty 3
A plane flies 1 350 km at an average speed of 540 km/h. It leaves at 09:40.
At what time does it land?

- A. 11:55
- B. 12:10
- C. 12:30
- D. 12:40

**Answer:** B

**Working:** Time $= 1350 \div 540 = 2.5$ h $= 2$ h 30 min.
$09{:}40 + 2 \text{ h } 30 \text{ min} = 12{:}10$.

C reads 2.5 h as 2 h 50 min, but 0.5 h is 30 minutes. A adds only 2 h 15 min,
and D adds 3 h.

### 4.10 | structured | 5 marks | difficulty 3
Shanice drives 90 km from her home to a town at an average speed of 60 km/h.
She stays for 40 minutes, then drives home along the same road at 45 km/h.

(a) How long does the outward journey take? **(1 mark)**

(b) How long does the return journey take? **(1 mark)**

(c) She leaves home at 08:15. At what time does she get back? **(1 mark)**

(d) Find her average speed for the driving only, both ways. **(2 marks)**

**Answer:** (a) 1 h 30 min (b) 2 h (c) 12:25 (d) 51.4 km/h (to 1 d.p.)

**Working:** (a) $90 \div 60 = 1.5$ h $= 1$ h 30 min.

(b) $90 \div 45 = 2$ h.

(c) Total time away: $1\text{ h }30 + 40 \text{ min} + 2 \text{ h} = 4$ h 10 min.
$08{:}15 + 4 \text{ h } 10 \text{ min} = 12{:}25$.

(d) Total distance $= 180$ km, total driving time $= 3.5$ h (1 mark).
$180 \div 3.5 = 51.43\ldots \approx 51.4$ km/h (1 mark). Not the average of 60
and 45, which is 52.5.
