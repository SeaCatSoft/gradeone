## Lesson

The **surface area** of a solid is the total area of all its faces — the
amount of wrapping paper that would cover it exactly. It's measured in
square units.

### Think in nets

A **net** is the flat shape you'd get by unfolding a solid. The surface area
of the solid is the area of its net. When a formula slips your mind, picture
the net and add up its pieces.

### Cube and cuboid

A cuboid has **six** rectangular faces, in three matching pairs: top and
bottom, front and back, left and right.

$$\text{SA of cuboid} = 2(lw + lh + wh)$$

A box 8 cm by 5 cm by 3 cm:

$$2(8 \times 5 + 8 \times 3 + 5 \times 3) = 2(40 + 24 + 15) = 2 \times 79 = 158 \text{ cm}^2$$

A cube has six identical square faces: $\text{SA} = 6a^2$.

### Prisms

A **prism** has the same cross-section all the way along. Its surface is:

- **two** copies of the cross-section (the ends), plus
- a rectangle for each side, all of length $l$.

Those side rectangles together unroll into one long rectangle whose width is
the **perimeter of the cross-section**:

$$\text{SA of prism} = 2 \times \text{area of cross-section} + \text{perimeter of cross-section} \times l$$

> A triangular prism has a right-angled triangle with sides 3 cm, 4 cm and
> 5 cm as its cross-section, and a length of 10 cm.

- Two triangles: $2 \times \frac{1}{2} \times 3 \times 4 = 12$ cm²
- Sides: $(3 + 4 + 5) \times 10 = 120$ cm²
- Total: $132$ cm²

### Cylinders

A cylinder is a prism with a circular cross-section. Its net is **two circles
and a rectangle**. The rectangle wraps around the side, so its width is the
circumference, $2\pi r$, and its height is $h$.

$$\text{SA of closed cylinder} = 2\pi r^2 + 2\pi r h$$

> A closed cylinder has radius 7 cm and height 10 cm. Use $\pi = \frac{22}{7}$.

- Two circles: $2 \times \frac{22}{7} \times 7^2 = 2 \times 154 = 308$ cm²
- Curved surface: $2 \times \frac{22}{7} \times 7 \times 10 = 440$ cm²
- Total: $748$ cm²

### Open or closed?

Read carefully. An **open** box or tank has no lid, so leave out one face. A
**pipe** or tube has no ends at all — only the curved surface. A cylinder
"open at one end" has one circle, not two.

## Flashcards

### 4.8 | What is surface area?
The total area of all the faces of a solid: the area of its net.

### 4.8 | Surface area of a cuboid?
$$2(lw + lh + wh)$$

Three pairs of matching rectangles.

### 4.8 | What shapes make up the net of a cylinder?
Two circles and one rectangle. The rectangle's width is the circumference,
$2\pi r$.

### 4.8 | Surface area of a closed cylinder?
$$2\pi r^2 + 2\pi r h$$

### 4.8 | How is the side area of a prism found quickly?
Perimeter of the cross-section × length of the prism.

### 4.8 | How does "open at the top" change a surface area calculation?
Leave out the top face. For a cylinder, use one circle instead of two.

## Questions

### 4.8 | numeric | 1 mark | difficulty 1
Find the surface area of a cube with edges of 5 cm, in cm².

**Answer:** 150

**Working:** Six faces, each $5 \times 5 = 25$ cm²: $6 \times 25 = 150$ cm².

### 4.8 | numeric | 1 mark | difficulty 2
A cuboid measures 10 cm by 6 cm by 4 cm. Find its surface area, in cm².

**Answer:** 248

**Working:** $2(10 \times 6 + 10 \times 4 + 6 \times 4) = 2(60 + 40 + 24) = 2 \times 124 = 248$ cm².

### 4.8 | mcq | 1 mark | difficulty 3
A cylindrical tin, **open at the top**, has radius 7 cm and height 12 cm. Using
$\pi = \frac{22}{7}$, what is its outside surface area?

- A. 528 cm²
- B. 682 cm²
- C. 836 cm²
- D. 1848 cm²

**Answer:** B

**Working:** One circle (the base): $\frac{22}{7} \times 49 = 154$ cm².
Curved surface: $2 \times \frac{22}{7} \times 7 \times 12 = 528$ cm².
Total: $154 + 528 = 682$ cm².

C counts two circles, as if the tin had a lid. A is only the curved surface.
D is the volume, $\pi r^2 h$.

### 4.8 | numeric | 1 mark | difficulty 3
A triangular prism has a cross-section that is a right-angled triangle with
sides 6 cm, 8 cm and 10 cm. The prism is 15 cm long. Find its total surface
area, in cm².

**Answer:** 408

**Working:** Two triangles: $2 \times \frac{1}{2} \times 6 \times 8 = 48$ cm².
Sides: perimeter $\times$ length $= (6 + 8 + 10) \times 15 = 360$ cm².
Total: $48 + 360 = 408$ cm².

### 4.8 | structured | 5 marks | difficulty 3
An open rectangular water tank (no lid) is 2 m long, 1.5 m wide and 1 m deep.
The inside is to be painted.

(a) Find the area of the base. **(1 mark)**

(b) Find the total area of the four walls. **(2 marks)**

(c) One tin of paint covers 4 m². How many tins are needed? **(2 marks)**

**Answer:** (a) 3 m² (b) 7 m² (c) 3 tins

**Working:** (a) $2 \times 1.5 = 3$ m².

(b) Two walls are $2 \times 1 = 2$ m² each, and two are $1.5 \times 1 = 1.5$ m²
each: $2(2) + 2(1.5) = 4 + 3 = 7$ m² (1 mark for the method, 1 for 7).

(c) Total to paint: $3 + 7 = 10$ m². $10 \div 4 = 2.5$, so **3** tins are needed
(1 mark for 10 m², 1 for rounding up to 3).
