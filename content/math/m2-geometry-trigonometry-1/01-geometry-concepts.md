## Lesson

Geometry has precise words. Using them exactly is half of every geometry mark.

### Points, lines and planes

- A **point** marks a position; it has no size.
- A **line** is straight and goes on forever in both directions.
- A **line segment** has two endpoints: $AB$.
- A **ray** starts at a point and goes on forever in one direction.
- A **plane** is a flat surface extending forever — like an endless tabletop.
- **Parallel** lines never meet; **perpendicular** lines meet at 90°.

### Angles

An angle measures a turn, in degrees. A full turn is 360°.

| Type | Size |
|---|---|
| Acute | less than 90° |
| Right | exactly 90° |
| Obtuse | between 90° and 180° |
| Straight | exactly 180° |
| Reflex | between 180° and 360° |

Two angles adding to 90° are **complementary**; two adding to 180° are
**supplementary**.

### Polygons

A **polygon** is a closed flat shape with straight sides. A **regular**
polygon has all sides equal **and** all angles equal.

| Sides | Name |
|---|---|
| 3 | triangle |
| 4 | quadrilateral |
| 5 | pentagon |
| 6 | hexagon |
| 7 | heptagon |
| 8 | octagon |
| 10 | decagon |

**Triangles** are named by sides (equilateral: 3 equal; isosceles: 2 equal;
scalene: none equal) or by angles (acute, right-angled, obtuse).

**Quadrilaterals** include the square, rectangle, rhombus (4 equal sides),
parallelogram (2 pairs of parallel sides), trapezium (1 pair of parallel sides)
and kite (2 pairs of equal adjacent sides).

### Circles

- **Radius:** centre to the edge. **Diameter:** edge to edge through the centre
  (twice the radius).
- **Chord:** a line segment joining two points on the circle.
- **Arc:** part of the circumference. **Sector:** the region between two radii.
- **Segment:** the region between a chord and its arc.
- **Tangent:** a line touching the circle at exactly one point.

### Geometry in the real world

Everyday objects are modelled by geometric shapes. A can of drink is a
**cylinder**, a road sign a **regular octagon**, the edge of a door and the
floor are **perpendicular**, and railway tracks are **parallel** lines.
Modelling like this is what lets you calculate — the volume of the can, the
area of the sign.

## Flashcards

### 4.1 | What is a reflex angle?
An angle between 180° and 360°.

### 4.1 | What do supplementary angles add up to?
180°.

### 4.1 | What makes a polygon regular?
All sides **and** all angles are equal.

### 4.1 | What is a chord?
A line segment joining two points on a circle.

### 4.1 | How does a trapezium differ from a parallelogram?
A trapezium has **one** pair of parallel sides; a parallelogram has **two**.

### 4.9 | What geometric shape models a tin of soup?
A **cylinder**.

## Questions

### 4.1 | mcq | 1 mark | difficulty 1
An angle of 205° is

- A. acute
- B. obtuse
- C. straight
- D. reflex

**Answer:** D

**Working:** It's between 180° and 360°.

### 4.1 | numeric | 1 mark | difficulty 1
What is the complement of an angle of 37°, in degrees?

**Answer:** 53

**Working:** Complementary angles add to 90°: $90 - 37 = 53$.

### 4.1 | mcq | 1 mark | difficulty 2
A quadrilateral has four equal sides but its angles are not right angles. What
is it?

- A. Square
- B. Rectangle
- C. Rhombus
- D. Trapezium

**Answer:** C

**Working:** A rhombus has four equal sides. A square would need right angles.

### 4.1 | structured | 3 marks | difficulty 2
(a) Name a polygon with 8 sides. **(1 mark)**

(b) What is the difference between a chord and a diameter? **(1 mark)**

(c) Name the geometric solid that best models a football. **(1 mark)**

**Answer:** (a) Octagon (b) A diameter is a chord that passes through the
centre; other chords don't. (c) A sphere
