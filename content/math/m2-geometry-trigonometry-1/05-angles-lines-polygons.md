## Lesson

Angle problems are solved with a small set of facts. The skill is spotting
which fact applies — and **stating it as your reason**, because CSEC gives marks
for reasons.

### Angles at a point and on a line

| Fact | Reason to write |
|---|---|
| Angles on a straight line add to 180° | "angles on a straight line" |
| Angles around a point add to 360° | "angles at a point" |
| Vertically opposite angles are equal | "vertically opposite angles" |

Vertically opposite angles are the pairs across from each other where two
straight lines cross.

| Pair | Relationship | Look for |
|---|---|---|
| **Corresponding** ($a$ and $b$) | equal | an **F** shape |
| **Alternate** ($b$ and $c$) | equal | a **Z** shape |
| **Co-interior** (allied) | add to 180° | a **C** or **U** shape |

These only work when the lines are **parallel** (shown by matching arrows).

### Triangles

- The angles in a triangle add to **180°**.
- An **exterior angle** equals the **sum of the two interior opposite angles**.
- In an **isosceles** triangle, the angles opposite the equal sides are equal.
- In an **equilateral** triangle, every angle is 60°.

### Polygons

A polygon with $n$ sides can be split into $n - 2$ triangles from one vertex, so

$$\text{sum of interior angles} = (n - 2) \times 180°$$

| Polygon | $n$ | Interior angle sum |
|---|---|---|
| Quadrilateral | 4 | 360° |
| Pentagon | 5 | 540° |
| Hexagon | 6 | 720° |

The **exterior** angles of any polygon add to **360°**. For a **regular**
polygon, each exterior angle is $\frac{360°}{n}$, and each interior angle is
$180°$ minus that.

> Find each interior angle of a regular octagon.

Exterior angle: $\frac{360°}{8} = 45°$. Interior angle: $180° - 45° = 135°$.

## Flashcards

### 4.6 | What do the angles on a straight line add up to?
180°.

### 4.6 | Are alternate angles equal or supplementary?
**Equal** (the Z shape), when the lines are parallel.

### 4.6 | What do co-interior angles add up to?
180° (the C or U shape).

### 4.6 | State the formula for the interior angle sum of an $n$-sided polygon.
$$(n - 2) \times 180°$$

### 4.6 | What do the exterior angles of any polygon add up to?
360°.

### 4.6 | What is the exterior angle of a triangle equal to?
The sum of the two interior opposite angles.

## Questions

### 4.6 | numeric | 1 mark | difficulty 1
Two angles in a triangle are 48° and 67°. Find the third angle, in degrees.

**Answer:** 65

**Working:** $180 - 48 - 67 = 65$ (angles in a triangle add to 180°).

### 4.6 | numeric | 1 mark | difficulty 2
Find the sum of the interior angles of a nonagon (9 sides), in degrees.

**Answer:** 1260

**Working:** $(9 - 2) \times 180° = 7 \times 180° = 1260°$.

### 4.6 | mcq | 1 mark | difficulty 2
A regular polygon has an interior angle of 150°. How many sides does it have?

- A. 10
- B. 12
- C. 15
- D. 30

**Answer:** B

**Working:** Exterior angle $= 180° - 150° = 30°$.
Number of sides $= 360° \div 30° = 12$.

### 4.6 | mcq | 1 mark | difficulty 2
Two parallel lines are crossed by a transversal. One of the co-interior angles
is 112°. What is the other?

- A. 22°
- B. 68°
- C. 112°
- D. 248°

**Answer:** B

**Working:** Co-interior angles add to 180°: $180° - 112° = 68°$.

### 4.6 | structured | 5 marks | difficulty 3
In triangle $ABC$, $AB = AC$ and angle $BAC = 40°$. The side $BC$ is extended to
$D$.

(a) Find angle $ABC$, giving a reason. **(2 marks)**

(b) Find angle $ACD$, giving a reason. **(2 marks)**

(c) Check your answer to (b) using a different fact. **(1 mark)**

**Answer:** (a) 70° (b) 110° (c) See working

**Working:** (a) The triangle is isosceles, so angles $ABC$ and $ACB$ are equal:
$\frac{180° - 40°}{2} = 70°$ (base angles of an isosceles triangle are equal;
angles in a triangle add to 180°).

(b) Angles on a straight line: $180° - 70° = 110°$.

(c) Exterior angle $=$ sum of interior opposite angles: $40° + 70° = 110°$. ✓
