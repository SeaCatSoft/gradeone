## Lesson

### The parts of a solid

- A **face** is a flat (or curved) surface.
- An **edge** is where two faces meet.
- A **vertex** (plural **vertices**) is a corner, where edges meet.

### Classes of solids

**Prisms** have the same cross-section all the way through, with two
identical, parallel end faces. They are named after the cross-section:
triangular prism, pentagonal prism. A cuboid is a rectangular prism; a cube is
a cuboid with all edges equal.

**Pyramids** have a polygon base and triangular faces meeting at a single
point, the **apex**. They are named after the base: square-based pyramid,
triangular pyramid (tetrahedron).

**Solids with curved surfaces:** the **cylinder** (a circular prism), the
**cone** (a circular pyramid) and the **sphere**.

### Counting faces, edges and vertices

| Solid | Faces | Edges | Vertices |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cube / cuboid | 6 | 12 | 8 |
| Triangular prism | 5 | 9 | 6 |
| Square-based pyramid | 5 | 8 | 5 |
| Tetrahedron | 4 | 6 | 4 |
| Pentagonal prism | 7 | 15 | 10 |

For a prism whose end is an $n$-sided polygon: $n + 2$ faces, $3n$ edges, $2n$
vertices. For a pyramid with an $n$-sided base: $n + 1$ faces, $2n$ edges,
$n + 1$ vertices.

### Euler's formula

For any solid with flat faces (a **polyhedron**):

$$F + V - E = 2$$

Check with a cube: $6 + 8 - 12 = 2$. ✓

The formula lets you find one number from the other two, and it's a good check
on your counting.

## Flashcards

### 4.6 | How many faces, edges and vertices does a cube have?
6 faces, 12 edges, 8 vertices.

### 4.6 | State Euler's formula for polyhedra.
$$F + V - E = 2$$

### 4.6 | What is the difference between a prism and a pyramid?
A prism has a constant cross-section with two parallel ends. A pyramid's faces
meet at a single apex.

### 4.6 | How many edges does a prism with a hexagonal cross-section have?
18. A prism with an $n$-sided end has $3n$ edges.

### 4.6 | What is a tetrahedron?
A triangular-based pyramid, with 4 triangular faces.

## Questions

### 4.6 | numeric | 1 mark | difficulty 1
How many vertices does a triangular prism have?

**Answer:** 6

**Working:** Three at each triangular end.

### 4.6 | numeric | 1 mark | difficulty 2
A polyhedron has 8 faces and 12 vertices. How many edges does it have?

**Answer:** 18

**Working:** $F + V - E = 2$: $8 + 12 - E = 2$, so $E = 18$.

### 4.6 | mcq | 1 mark | difficulty 2
Which solid has 5 faces, 8 edges and 5 vertices?

- A. Cube
- B. Triangular prism
- C. Square-based pyramid
- D. Tetrahedron

**Answer:** C

**Working:** A square base plus 4 triangles is 5 faces; 4 base edges plus 4
slanting edges is 8; 4 base corners plus the apex is 5 vertices.

### 4.6 | structured | 3 marks | difficulty 2
A pyramid has a base that is a hexagon.

(a) How many faces does it have? **(1 mark)**

(b) How many edges does it have? **(1 mark)**

(c) Verify Euler's formula for this pyramid. **(1 mark)**

**Answer:** (a) 7 (b) 12 (c) $F + V - E = 7 + 7 - 12 = 2$ ✓

**Working:** It has $6 + 1 = 7$ vertices: six around the base and the apex.
