## Lesson

A shape has **symmetry** when part of it is a copy of another part, arranged in
a regular way. There are two types for flat shapes.

### Line symmetry (reflective)

A **line of symmetry** divides a shape into two halves that are **mirror
images**. Fold along it, and the halves match exactly.

| Shape | Lines of symmetry |
|---|---|
| Isosceles triangle | 1 |
| Equilateral triangle | 3 |
| Rectangle | 2 (not the diagonals!) |
| Square | 4 |
| Rhombus | 2 (the diagonals) |
| Kite | 1 |
| Parallelogram (not a rhombus or rectangle) | 0 |
| Regular $n$-sided polygon | $n$ |
| Circle | infinitely many |

The rectangle catches people out: folding along a diagonal doesn't make the
halves line up.

### Rotational symmetry

A shape has **rotational symmetry** if it looks the same after being turned
through less than a full turn about its centre.

The **order** of rotational symmetry is the number of times it looks the same
in one full turn.

- A square looks the same 4 times: order **4**.
- A parallelogram looks the same twice (after 180° and 360°): order **2**.
- A shape that only matches after a full 360° turn has order 1 — which is
  described as **no** rotational symmetry.

A regular polygon with $n$ sides has rotational symmetry of order $n$. Each turn
is $\frac{360°}{n}$: for a regular hexagon, $60°$.

### Using both

A parallelogram has **no** lines of symmetry but rotational symmetry of order
2. A kite has 1 line of symmetry but no rotational symmetry. Questions often ask
for both — answer each separately.

## Flashcards

### 4.5 | How many lines of symmetry does a rectangle have?
**Two** — through the midpoints of opposite sides. Not the diagonals.

### 4.5 | What is the order of rotational symmetry of a square?
4.

### 4.5 | How many lines of symmetry does a regular pentagon have?
5.

### 4.5 | Does a parallelogram have line symmetry?
Not in general. It has rotational symmetry of order 2, but no lines of symmetry.

### 4.5 | What does "order of rotational symmetry" mean?
The number of times a shape looks the same during one full turn.

## Questions

### 4.5 | numeric | 1 mark | difficulty 1
How many lines of symmetry does a regular hexagon have?

**Answer:** 6

**Working:** A regular polygon with $n$ sides has $n$ lines of symmetry.

### 4.5 | mcq | 1 mark | difficulty 2
Which shape has rotational symmetry of order 2 but **no** lines of symmetry?

- A. Rectangle
- B. Parallelogram
- C. Kite
- D. Isosceles trapezium

**Answer:** B

**Working:** A parallelogram matches itself after a half-turn, but no fold makes
its halves coincide. A rectangle has 2 lines of symmetry as well; a kite and an
isosceles trapezium have a line of symmetry but no rotational symmetry.

### 4.5 | numeric | 1 mark | difficulty 2
A regular polygon has rotational symmetry of order 8. Through what angle, in
degrees, must it be turned to look the same for the first time?

**Answer:** 45

**Working:** $360° \div 8 = 45°$.

### 4.5 | structured | 3 marks | difficulty 2
For each shape, state the number of lines of symmetry and the order of
rotational symmetry.

(a) An equilateral triangle **(1 mark)**

(b) A rhombus **(1 mark)**

(c) A kite **(1 mark)**

**Answer:** (a) 3 lines; order 3 (b) 2 lines; order 2 (c) 1 line; order 1 (no
rotational symmetry)
