## Lesson

Accurate drawing is a practical skill: it's learnt with a pencil, a ruler and a
protractor in your hands. This lesson covers the technique and the usual
errors. Examiners typically allow **±1 mm** for lengths and **±1°** for angles.

### Your instruments

- a **sharp** pencil (a blunt one adds a millimetre to every line)
- a **ruler** marked in cm and mm
- a **protractor**, for measuring and drawing angles
- a pair of **compasses**, for arcs and circles (next lesson)

### Drawing and measuring lines

Put the **0 mark** — not the end of the ruler — exactly on the starting point.
Read the length at the other end in cm and mm: 6.4 cm is 6 cm and 4 mm.

### Measuring an angle

1. Put the protractor's **centre point** exactly on the **vertex** (the corner).
2. Line up the **base line** (0°) with one arm of the angle.
3. Follow the scale that **starts at 0** on that arm, round to the other arm.
4. **Sense-check:** is the angle acute or obtuse? A protractor has two scales,
   and reading the wrong one gives, say, 130° instead of 50°. If it looks
   acute, the answer must be less than 90°.

If the arms are too short to reach the scale, extend them lightly with a ruler
first.

### Drawing an angle

To draw 65° at point $A$ on a line $AB$:

1. Put the centre of the protractor on $A$, base line along $AB$.
2. Using the scale that starts at 0 on $AB$, make a small dot at 65°.
3. Remove the protractor and rule a line from $A$ through the dot.
4. Label the angle.

### Reflex angles

A protractor only goes to 180°. To draw or measure a **reflex** angle, work with
the **other** angle: $360° - $ reflex. To draw 290°, draw $360 - 290 = 70°$, and
mark the reflex angle on the outside.

## Flashcards

### 4.3 | Where must the protractor's centre point go?
Exactly on the **vertex** of the angle.

### 4.3 | How do you avoid reading the wrong scale on a protractor?
Use the scale that starts at 0 on one arm, and check whether the angle looks
acute or obtuse.

### 4.2 | How do you draw a reflex angle of 250°?
Draw $360° - 250° = 110°$, then mark the reflex angle on the outside.

### 4.2 | Where do you start measuring on a ruler?
From the **0 mark**, not the end of the ruler.

### 4.3 | What accuracy is usually expected for lengths and angles?
Within about 1 mm and 1°.

## Questions

### 4.3 | mcq | 1 mark | difficulty 1
A student measures an obviously acute angle and reads 140° from the protractor.
What has most likely gone wrong?

- A. The protractor was upside down.
- B. The wrong scale was read; the angle is 40°.
- C. The angle is really reflex.
- D. The arms are too long.

**Answer:** B

**Working:** An acute angle must be under 90°. $180° - 140° = 40°$ — the reading
from the other scale.

### 4.2 | numeric | 1 mark | difficulty 2
To draw a reflex angle of 305°, what angle should be drawn with the protractor,
in degrees?

**Answer:** 55

**Working:** $360° - 305° = 55°$. Draw 55°, then mark the reflex angle outside it.

### 4.3 | mcq | 1 mark | difficulty 1
A line is measured from the 0 mark to the reading "8 cm and 7 small divisions".
How long is it?

- A. 8.07 cm
- B. 8.7 cm
- C. 87 cm
- D. 15 cm

**Answer:** B

**Working:** Each small division is 1 mm $= 0.1$ cm, so 8 cm and 7 mm is 8.7 cm.

### 4.2 | structured | 3 marks | difficulty 2
Describe, step by step, how to draw an angle of 75° at the point $P$ on a line
$PQ$. **(3 marks)**

**Answer:** Place the protractor's centre on $P$ with its base line along $PQ$
(1 mark); mark a dot at 75° on the scale starting at 0 on $PQ$ (1 mark); remove
the protractor and rule a line from $P$ through the dot (1 mark).
